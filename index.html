What Merger is

A deal desk built where your deals actually happen: in chat.

Deals don't come in through a CRM. They come in as a WhatsApp forward at 6am, a Telegram group ping, a LinkedIn DM from someone you met once. By the time you've copied it somewhere trackable, three more have landed on three other apps.

Merger runs on your own Matrix-based infrastructure — a private, self-hosted messaging core that bridges all twelve networks natively. You keep your existing numbers and accounts. Counterparties never know anything changed.

On top of that core, Merger layers what a dealmaker actually needs: AI triage that reads inbound and classifies it by vertical, size, and urgency; a Rolodex that builds itself from your conversations; e-signature tracking for DocuSign and Dotloop envelopes; and Merger Match — a counterparty discovery network that turns a mutual match into a live deal room in one tap.

The stack

Six things your group chats were never going to do for you.

Unified inbox

Twelve networks, one thread view

Every conversation from every bridged platform in a single channel rail — searchable, filterable, and reply-from-anywhere. Your WhatsApp number stays your WhatsApp number.

AI triage

Inbound reads itself

Every new message is scored and classified — vertical, deal size, counterparty intent, urgency. Real deal flow rises to the top; noise settles to the bottom. You skim a pipeline, not an inbox.

Merger Match

Counterparty discovery

A swipeable network of verified buyers, sellers, and brokers across six verticals. A mutual match opens a live deal room instantly — contact created, deal card generated, provenance logged.

Rolodex

Contacts that build themselves

Every counterparty across every network resolves to one profile: platforms, deal history, verticals, notes. No data entry — your Rolodex assembles itself from the conversations you're already having.

Signing

Paper tracked next to the talk

DocuSign and Dotloop envelopes surface inside the deal room they belong to. See who's signed, who's stalling, and nudge them on the channel they actually answer.
